TP B: Text Analysis

Analytical Exposition Text & Generic Structure

Analytical Exposition adalah teks yang memaparkan argumen penulis mengenai suatu isu penting. Bacalah contoh teks di bawah dengan cermat!

🔵 Thesis (Pernyataan Pendapat Utama) 🟢 Arguments (Alasan & Bukti Pendukung) 🟠 Reiteration (Penegasan Ulang Kesimpulan)

Why Physical Activity is Essential for High School Students

[1] THESIS STATEMENT

In this modern digital era, many high school students spend long hours sitting in front of laptops and smartphones, leading to a dangerous sedentary lifestyle. I strongly believe that regular physical activity is essential for high school students to maintain physical and mental well-being.

(Penjelasan: Paragraph ini memperkenalkan isu gaya hidup kurang gerak dan menegaskan pendapat utama penulis/Thesis bahwa olahraga itu wajib bagi siswa).

[2] ARGUMENT 1

Firstly, engaging in physical activity boosts the immune system and prevents chronic illness. When teenagers exercise regularly, their body burns excess calories and regulates blood circulation; therefore, they become less vulnerable to obesity and cardiovascular issues.

[3] ARGUMENT 2

Secondly, regular exercise enhances mental health and academic concentration. Physical movement releases endorphins which reduce stress levels. As a result, students who stay physically active study more effectively and avoid chronic fatigue.

[4] REITERATION

In conclusion, physical activity is not merely an optional pastime, but an urgent necessity for high school students. Therefore, teenagers should schedule at least 30 minutes of exercise daily to secure a healthy future.

(Penjelasan: Paragraph penutup yang menyimpulkan dan menegaskan ulang posisi penulis).

Language Features (Ciri Kebahasaan)

1. Simple Present Tense

Menggunakan kata kerja bentuk pertama (V1) untuk menyatakan fakta umum.

"Exercise boosts immune system."

2. Causal Conjunctions (Konjungsi Kausal)

Kata hubung sebab-akibat untuk menghubungkan argumen.

because, therefore, as a result, strictly due to

3. Modals

Kata kerja bantu untuk menunjukkan tingkat saran / keharusan.

should, must, ought to, can

4. Thinking / Mental Verbs

Kata kerja yang mengekspresikan pemikiran atau perasaan penulis.

I believe, I think, I feel, researchers agree
